# FloodLens — Limitations, Assumptions & Operational Scope

This document details known operational boundaries, data uncertainties, and engineering assumptions governing FloodLens.

---

## 1. System Scope Boundaries

1. **Vulnerability Assessment vs. Real-Time Emergency Warning:**
   - FloodLens is designed for **spatial vulnerability benchmarking, planning, and risk-aware route comparison**.
   - It does **not** provide dynamic riverine surge warnings, automated barrier closures, or sensor-linked telemetry.
2. **Static vs. Dynamic Exposure:**
   - Vulnerability scores reflect baseline topographic and historical risk. Flash flood inundation depths during extreme precipitation events (e.g., >100 mm/hour cloudbursts) depend heavily on dynamic localized blockages not represented in static geospatial layers.

---

## 2. Geospatial & Data Uncertainties

1. **DEM Spatial Resolution (30 meters):**
   - The Copernicus DEM GLO-30 provides a 30m $\times$ 30m pixel resolution. While excellent for regional slope and valley depression modeling, it does not capture micro-topographic urban structures such as road curbs, underpass dips under 15m wide, flyover ramps, or storm drain culvert grates.
2. **Reporting Bias in Historical Flood Records:**
   - BBMP and crowdsourced flood logs often reflect population density and smartphone usage rather than uniform geographical reporting. High-density commercial districts (e.g. Koramangala, Indiranagar, Bellandur) tend to report more incidents than peripheral rural-urban fringes.
3. **Subsurface Drainage Infrastructure:**
   - Underground pipe diameters, pump station operational statuses, silt accumulation levels, and encroached rajakaluve sections are not fully digitized in open datasets and therefore cannot be accounted for dynamically.

---

## 3. Navigational Safety Advisory Disclaimer

- **Never drive, walk, or cycle through floodwaters:** "Turn Around, Don't Drown." Water depth and road surface integrity cannot be guaranteed during active heavy rain events.
- Route recommendations generated by FloodLens are illustrative comparisons based on mapped historical vulnerability and should always be superseded by on-ground traffic police advisories, NDMA/BBMP alerts, and physical road conditions.
